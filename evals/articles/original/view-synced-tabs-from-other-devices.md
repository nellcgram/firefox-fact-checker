# View synced tabs from other devices

Firefox Sync lets you access open tabs across your desktop, laptop and mobile devices that use Firefox.

Before you begin, set up Sync on all devices that use Firefox. See [How do I set up](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/how-do-i-set-sync-my-computer) [Sync on my computer?](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/how-do-i-set-sync-my-computer) for more information. Make sure that **Open tabs** are checked in the Sync settings.

## View your synced tabs

There are several ways to access your tabs from other devices on desktop:

- Click the menu button and then select your Mozilla account name. All synced tabs will appear right under the Sign out… menu item in the Mozilla account panel.

- [Use the Firefox Sidebar](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/use-sidebar-access-tools-and-vertical-tabs) to access synced tabs. Select the laptop and phone button in the sidebar to view and manage the tabs.

- Open [Firefox View](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/how-set-tab-pickup-firefox-view) from the Tools menu in the Firefox Menu bar or by clicking the List all tabs icon at the end of the tab strip and selecting View All Tabs, then select Tabs from other devices.

## Manage your synced tabs

Here's what you can do with your synced tabs:

- To open a tab, just click it.

- To close a tab on a synced device, click the cross ✕ button next to it (in the sidebar, you'll have to hover over the tab for this button to appear). To undo the closing, select the **Undo** button that appears.

- To see more options, right-click a tab if you use the sidebar or click the three-dot button next to the tab if you use Firefox View.

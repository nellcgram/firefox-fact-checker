# Use the sidebar to access tools and vertical tabs

The Firefox sidebar lets you quickly access multiple tools in one click, without leaving your main view, including:

- an [AI chatbot](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/ai-chatbot) of your choice

- bookmarks

- history

- tabs from devices you sync with your [Mozilla account](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/access-mozilla-services-firefox-account)

- passwords

You can also try out vertical tabs. If you turn on vertical tabs, your pinned and open tabs will appear in the sidebar instead of along the top of the browser. Early testers report feeling more organized after using vertical tabs for a few days.

## Show the Firefox sidebar

1. In the Menu bar at the top of the screen, click Firefox and select Settings (or Preferences, in some cases). The Firefox Settings tab will open.

2. Select Tabs and browsing on the left and go to the Browser layout section.

3. Select the Show sidebar option.

After you enable the Firefox sidebar, a sidebar button is added to your toolbar. The sidebar with shortcuts to tools is shown on the left; you can move it to the right. To hide and bring the sidebar back into view, click the sidebar button in the toolbar.

## Move the sidebar to the left or right

The sidebar shows up on the left by default, but you can also move it to the right.

1. Right-click (or hold down the Control key while you click) the sidebar button.

2. Select Customize Sidebar from the menu.

3. In the Sidebar settings panel, check **Move Sidebar to the right**.

> **Tip:** You can also customize the location of the sidebar button by clicking the Firefox menu button, then clicking More tools and selecting Customize Toolbar… The toolbar panel will now become customizable, so you can adjust the location of the sidebar button by dragging and dropping it in your preferred location. To learn more about toolbar customization, see [Customize Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/customize-firefox-controls-buttons-and-toolbars) [controls, buttons and toolbars](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/customize-firefox-controls-buttons-and-toolbars).

## Turn on vertical tabs

Right-click (or hold down the Control key while you click) on either the toolbar or tab strip. Then, select Turn on Vertical Tabs from the menu.

> **Note:** When vertical tabs are turned on, you can access the [Tab overview menu](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/tab-overview-menu) from the List all tabs icon in the toolbar.

After you’ve turned on vertical tabs, you can turn them off from the same context menus or from the Customize sidebar settings.

## Customize sidebar and vertical tabs

After you enable the sidebar or turn on vertical tabs, you can customize them anytime.

To display the Customize sidebar panel, click the gear icon at the bottom of the sidebar. Here’s what you can customize:

- Settings for vertical tabs

- The location of the sidebar, which you can position on either the left or right of your screen.

- The tools displayed in the sidebar. Uncheck a checkbox to remove a tool from the sidebar, and check a checkbox to add it.

- Turn vertical tabs on and off by checking the **Vertical tabs** checkbox. After you turn on vertical tabs, switch between a minimalistic collapsed view and expanded view, which shows the tab titles using the sidebar button in the toolbar or using the Ctrl + Z shortcut.

- If you prefer to keep your tabs and sidebar hidden, select the **Hide tabs and sidebar** checkbox. This can provide more room on screen and more focus.

## Set up sidebar tools

Some tools, like the AI chatbot and viewing tabs from other devices, require additional setup.

**Set up your preferred chatbot in the sidebar**

Select the sparkle icon in the sidebar and choose a chatbot. [Learn more about AI](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/ai-chatbot) [Chatbots in Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/ai-chatbot).

**Manage tabs from devices you sync with your Mozilla account** To view and manage tabs from your synced devices, first sign in to your Mozilla account on those devices (phones, other computers, tablets).

Select the laptop and phone button in the sidebar to view and manage tabs that are open on your synced devices.

To close tabs on a synced device, hover over a tab and select the ✕ button to close it. This closes the tab on the synced device. To undo the closing, select the **Undo** button that appears.

**View and search browsing history** Select the clock button in the sidebar or use Command + Shift + H shortcut to view your history in the sidebar.

**View and manage bookmarks** Select the star button in the sidebar or use Command + B shortcut to view your bookmarks in the sidebar.

Bookmark any tab by dragging it from your tab strip (vertical or horizontal) to the open bookmarks panel.

**Manage passwords from the sidebar in Firefox** You can access your passwords from the Firefox sidebar, making it easier to manage your stored logins without opening the Firefox about: logins page.

**Enable passwords in the Firefox sidebar**

1. Click the Customize sidebar icon at the bottom of the sidebar.

2. In the **Tools** menu, enable **Passwords** in the sidebar.

**Use the sidebar password manager** Once enabled, a new Passwords icon appears in the sidebar. Click the icon to open the password manager, where you can view, search, edit saved passwords and more:

- Add new login credentials

- Import passwords from another browser or a file

- Export saved passwords

- Remove all saved passwords

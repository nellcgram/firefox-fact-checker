# Access AI chatbots in Firefox

Windows 10 reached EOS (end of support) on October 14, 2025. If you are on Windows 10, see [**this article**](https://support.mozilla.org/kb/firefox-support-windows-10-end-support).

You have the option to use an AI chatbot of your choice in the [updated sidebar](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/use-sidebar-access-tools-and-vertical-tabs). The sidebar allows you to keep a variety of browser tools, including a chatbot, in view as you browse. You can choose from the following chatbot providers: Anthropic Claude, ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Mistral Vibe.

Each provider can assist with tasks such as summarizing what you’re reading, brainstorming ideas, or helping you write. [Learn more about each provider](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/ai-chatbot#w_learn-about-chatbot-providers).

Learn [what to keep in mind when using AI chatbots](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/ai-chatbot#w_what-to-keep-in-mind-when-using-ai-chatbots), how to [select a chatbot provider](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/ai-chatbot#w_how-to-select-a-chatbot-provider-in-the-sidebar), how to [switch providers](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/ai-chatbot#w_how-to-change-chatbot-providers) and how to [remove the AI chatbot shortcut from your Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/ai-chatbot#w_how-to-remove-the-ai-chatbot-shortcut-from-your-firefox-sidebar) [sidebar](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/ai-chatbot#w_how-to-remove-the-ai-chatbot-shortcut-from-your-firefox-sidebar).

## What to keep in mind when using AI chatbots

If you choose to use AI chatbots – whether that’s in Firefox, as an app, or in another browser – keep these things in mind:

- When you use a chatbot, you are agreeing to that provider’s privacy policies and terms of use. Each chatbot provider has their own terms of use and privacy policies. View the [privacy policies and terms for providers in Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/ai-chatbot#w_learn-about-chatbot-providers).

- You should verify any information you get from AI chatbots. AI chatbots are powered by generative AI which, in basic terms, predicts likely text or images based on prompts. It’s not designed or guaranteed to provide definitive facts. More about [how AI chatbots work at a high level](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/ai-chatbot#w_how-ai-chatbots-work).

- Some chatbots are more privacy-respecting than others. To learn more about protecting your privacy when you use chatbots, [follow these helpful tips from the](https://foundation.mozilla.org/en/privacynotincluded/articles/how-to-protect-your-privacy-from-chatgpt-and-other-ai-chatbots/) [Mozilla Foundation](https://foundation.mozilla.org/en/privacynotincluded/articles/how-to-protect-your-privacy-from-chatgpt-and-other-ai-chatbots/).

## Learn about chatbot providers

For up-to-date information about each chatbot and what it offers, each provider’s website is the best place to look. Most chatbots are experimental and frequently change how they work, how they look, and what they offer.

If you’re comparing, you might consider whether the chatbot:

- Can generate in the format you need, like text, image or code

- Can analyze files, like documents and images

- Is free or paid

- Requires an account (may vary by task and country)

- Allows you to adjust settings — for example, whether your chats and information are shared with the provider
